Elevation tells the reader what is sitting above what. NachUI keeps it to three levels, defined as tokens in both modes and mapped to `shadow-sm`, `shadow-md` and `shadow-lg`. Anything else, like `shadow-xl`, `shadow-2xl` or an arbitrary shadow, is off the table.

## The three levels

Rendered with the values from the token files, on the real background, card and popover colors of each mode.

<ElevationScale />

| Utility     | Use                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `shadow-sm` | Inline controls that sit slightly above their track, like the active tab pill or a switch thumb |
| `shadow-md` | Anchored floating surfaces: popover, hover card, dropdown and context menu, select, toast       |
| `shadow-lg` | Modal surfaces: dialog, sheet, drawer                                                           |

The rule of thumb: the further something is from the page, and the more it blocks, the bigger its shadow. Something anchored to a trigger gets `md`; something that takes over the screen gets `lg`.

## Values

<ElevationValues />

Light mode shadows are tinted with the same warm hue as the neutrals and stay soft. Dark mode shadows are pure black and much stronger.

## Cards stay flat

Cards and other containers that sit in the flow of the page have no shadow. They are separated by `bg-card` and a `border`, which is enough on a calm page and keeps shadows meaningful: if something casts one, it is floating.

```tsx
<div className="bg-card border-border rounded-lg border p-6">Flat, in the flow</div>
<div className="bg-popover border-border rounded-md border p-4 shadow-md">Floating</div>
```

## Dark mode

On a dark page a shadow alone is hard to see, so dark mode lifts a surface in two ways at once: a lighter fill and a stronger shadow. The surfaces step up in lightness as they rise (background 14.5%, surface muted 18.2%, card 21.8%, popover 23.9%), which is why every floating surface must use `bg-popover`. A menu painted with `bg-background` would have the same color as the page under it and lose its edge.
